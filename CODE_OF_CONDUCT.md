# Code of conduct

Inkhue is a small, friendly project. Everyone is welcome here — whatever your experience, background or language.
Questions in Chinese, English or any other language are fine.

**Please**
- be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source;
- assume good intent, and critique ideas, not people;
- share real results honestly, including the ones that didn't work.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private information.

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through
[a security advisory](https://github.com/TonyD365/Inkhue/security/advisories/new) or by email on the maintainer's
GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content or block people who break these rules.
